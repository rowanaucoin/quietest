# QuietQuest — Business Case

## 1. The idea
StFX has a relatively small campus compared to its large student population, which makes finding a quiet study space difficult. Midterms and final exams are already stressful enough without having to spend extra time looking for somewhere to study. During the colder months, students may spend up to an hour walking around campus in freezing temperatures, only to find that most study spaces are already busy. QuietQuest aims to solve this problem by making it easier for students to find a suitable place to study.

QuietQuest allows students to compare noise and crowd levels before leaving their rooms. Instead of walking around campus or texting friends to see which areas are busy, students can find this information in one convenient location. This saves time, reduces stress, and allows students to focus more on their schoolwork.

QuietQuest would be free for students to use. The business would make money by charging universities a subscription fee to access anonymous, combined information about study-space usage. This could help universities understand which areas are overcrowded and where additional study spaces may be needed. This business model would provide value to both students and universities.

## 2. Where IT creates value
Technology allows QuietQuest to collect student reports and automatically update QuietScores. This makes it easier for students to check how quiet or busy a study space is. Right now, the website uses sample data stored in the user's browser, but a real version would allow students to share and view updated information from anywhere.

QuietQuest uses technology to improve operations and customer service. The website automatically updates QuietScores when students submit reports. This saves time and reduces the amount of work needed to run the website. It also helps students find quiet study spaces faster. As more students use QuietQuest, technology could help keep business costs low.

## 3. Network effects
Network effects happen when a product or service becomes more valuable as more people use it. QuietQuest would benefit from network effects because it depends on students sharing information with each other. The more students who submit reports, the more information will be available about different study spaces around campus. This would make it easier for students to find a quiet place to study. If only a few students use QuietQuest, there may not be enough updated information to make the website useful. QuietQuest uses a one-sided network effect because students both share and use information. As more students report noise and crowd levels, the website becomes more useful for other students. This could encourage even more students to use QuietQuest.

One of the biggest challenges for QuietQuest would be getting its first users. Students may not want to use the website if there are not enough reports. This is known as the cold-start problem. QuietQuest could attract its first users through social media, student groups, and posters around campus. Midterms and finals would be a good time to promote the website because more students are looking for quiet places to study.

Another important part of QuietQuest would be getting students to continue submitting reports. The reporting process is designed to be quick and easy. Students may also want to help each other because they understand how annoying it is to walk around campus looking for somewhere to study. As more students find the website helpful, they may recommend it to their friends.

However, network effects can also create problems. Some students might submit false reports, or information could become outdated. If students keep arriving at study spaces that are busier than expected, they may stop trusting QuietQuest. A real version of the website could reduce this problem by removing old reports and allowing students to flag incorrect information.

The QuietQuest prototype demonstrates network effects through an interactive dashboard. Users can move a slider from 0 to 200 sample reports and see how the number of study spaces covered increases. The dashboard assumes that every 20 reports provides coverage for one additional study space, up to 10 spaces. These numbers are made up to demonstrate how the business could grow in the future. They do not represent actual student activity at StFX. The current prototype also does not share reports between different users, which would require a shared online database in the future.

## 4. Can it be copied?
Imitation resistance means making it difficult for other businesses to copy a business and become just as successful. One challenge for QuietQuest is that competitors could easily create a similar website. Features such as QuietScores, student reports, and study-space listings are not extremely difficult to copy. With AI tools becoming more common, building a similar website would not take much time. Because of this, QuietQuest cannot depend on its technology alone to stay ahead of competitors.

What would be more difficult to copy is the student community that QuietQuest could build over time. If many students regularly use the website and submit reports, a new competitor would have to build its own community from the beginning. QuietQuest could also collect information over time about which study spaces are usually busy and when they are most popular. This information could help both students and universities make better decisions.

The VRIN framework can be used to understand whether QuietQuest could develop a long-term competitive advantage. VRIN stands for Valuable, Rare, Inimitable, and Non-substitutable.

Valuable: QuietQuest is valuable because it solves a problem that many students face. It helps students save time and reduces the stress of finding somewhere to study. This is especially important during midterms and finals. It could also provide value to universities by helping them understand which study spaces are overcrowded.

Rare: The website itself is not rare because other businesses could create something similar. However, a large collection of updated information about study spaces at StFX could become rare. This information would be more useful than general information about campus buildings because it would show how busy and quiet different areas are at different times.

Inimitable: Competitors could copy the design and features of QuietQuest, but it would be harder to copy its student community, past reports, and relationships with universities. Building trust with students takes time. If QuietQuest becomes a website that students regularly depend on, new competitors may struggle to attract those same users.

Non-substitutable: QuietQuest does not fully meet this requirement because students have other ways of finding study spaces. They could text friends, walk around campus, or use a university booking system. However, QuietQuest could become a preferred option because it combines noise and crowd information in one place.

To make the business harder to copy, QuietQuest could build strong relationships with universities and student groups. It could also improve its website based on student feedback and make sure reports remain useful and updated. These steps could help build trust and give students more reasons to continue using the website.

Currently, QuietQuest does not have a strong competitive advantage because it is only a prototype that uses sample data. It does not have an active student community or official partnerships with universities. However, if the business grows and gains regular users, it could develop resources that are harder for competitors to copy. Its biggest long-term advantage would come from its community, information, and relationships rather than the website itself.

A student community would be valuable because students provide useful reports. It could be rare and difficult to copy because building trust takes time. However, students could still ask their friends.

Past reports would be valuable because they show when study spaces are busiest. This data could be rare and difficult to copy because it takes time to collect. However, universities could collect their own information.

University relationships would be valuable because they help attract users and generate revenue. Strong partnerships could be rare and difficult to copy. However, competitors could build their own relationships. Even with a large budget, developing these three resources could take months or years.


## 5. Cloud and operations
QuietQuest currently uses GitHub Pages to keep its website online. This allows students to access the website from their phones or computers without having to download anything. GitHub Pages is also free, which helps keep the costs of building the prototype low.

In the future, QuietQuest would need an online database to store student reports. Currently, reports are only saved on the device being used. An online database would allow all students to see the same updated information. Cloud technology would also help QuietQuest handle more users as the business grows and possibly expands to other universities.

The daily operations of QuietQuest would include fixing website problems, removing old reports, and making improvements based on student feedback. The business would also need to manage the costs of keeping the website online and storing information. These costs could increase as more students and universities begin using the website.

QuietQuest would use PaaS for its online database and SaaS for monitoring website problems. If the cloud provider stopped working, students could view the last saved information. If prices increased, QuietQuest could compare other providers. The project log explains what a real version would need.

## 6. Risks and ethics
Protecting student privacy would also be important. QuietQuest would not need to collect personal information such as student names or student numbers. Universities would only receive combined information about how busy study spaces are. This would allow universities to make better decisions without knowing which students submitted reports.

There are several risks that QuietQuest could face. Some students might submit fake reports, while others might stop submitting reports altogether. The website could also experience problems during midterms and finals when many students are using it. Another risk is that universities may not be willing to pay for the service.

QuietQuest could lower these risks by advertising around campus, removing outdated reports, and allowing students to flag false information. Using reliable cloud services could also help prevent website problems during busy periods. QuietQuest could offer universities a free trial to show them how the information could be useful before asking them to pay for a subscription.
