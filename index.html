How QuietQuest works

QuietQuest uses simulated noise and crowd ratings to calculate a QuietScore from 0 to 100. Lower noise and crowd levels produce a higher score. Scores of 80–100 are Very Quiet, 50–79 are Moderate, and 0–49 are Noisy. All spaces and values on this page are simulated student prototype data.
